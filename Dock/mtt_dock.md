# mtt_dock

## What it is
A waveform strip for a long horizontal docker whose content follows REAPER's selection. With exactly one item selected it shows the whole source of the active take with the played part highlighted: dragging the highlight slips the content without moving or resizing the item, a double-click moves the item window onto a take found in the file (or centres it on the clicked point), and any other zone of the file can be selected and dragged onto the arrange as a new item on the same file. Otherwise it shows a gated loopback recording of the master, kept by mtt_dock_recorder.jsfx (last in the master chain, all master channels, only sound above a threshold), with the sounds inside it found from onset to tail: it can be selected (left-drag, draggable edges, double-click on a sound) and dragged onto the arrange as a new, selected item (cut at the sound's first sample when the selection starts on a detected sound), stereo by default or mono / original with Cmd held. Holding the right button listens in both views (the item through its track and FX, the recording through the master output). With several projects open, each recorder keeps its own recording and records only while its project is active; the dock shows the active project's one. A gear in the bottom right corner opens the settings in a floating window. Requires ReaImGui and SWS.
