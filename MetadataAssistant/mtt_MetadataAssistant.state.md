Also in atexit. After a project switch the snapshot is re-read, so nothing is written into the new project. A dim line under the settings says so.
  - 0.22: the pre-FX take is scanned in blocks. Long items use up to three time-mapped excerpts within the detailed-analysis budget. Detailed DSP uses up to 48 kHz and separate channel power. The listening proxy is 16 kHz mono with cancellation-aware folding. Item and active levels, event timing, local attacks and decays, tonal evolution, band balance and stereo motion reach the card and the drafting model. Measuring can be stopped while the window stays interactive.
