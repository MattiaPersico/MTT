## What it is

Mirrors the layout of the selected items on the highest selected track onto the selected items of the other tracks: each other track keeps its first selected item, which is split into as many pieces as there are reference items (overlapping references are merged first), each piece positioned on the corresponding reference item while carrying over the source material, start offset, volume, pitch and playrate.
