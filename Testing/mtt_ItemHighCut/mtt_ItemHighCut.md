# mtt_ItemHighCut

## What it is
An action for selected items: it puts a gentle high cut (low-pass, 6 dB/oct by default or 12 dB/oct, cutoff default 20 kHz) on the active take's FX chain and shows the take envelope of the cutoff, so the user can lower and raise the cutoff by drawing on the item. The JSFX ships beside the script and is copied into REAPER's Effects/MTT folder on first run.
